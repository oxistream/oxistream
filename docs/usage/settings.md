# Settings

| Section | Contents | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Playback | Codec, maximum and start quality, default audio track, see [quality, codec and audio](watching.md#quality-codec-and-audio) | Codec and quality signed in; audio everyone |
| Home page | Rows to show and their order, see [home](browsing.md#home) | Signed in |
| History | Pause, retention, clear, see [history](library.md#history) | Signed in |
| SponsorBlock | Per-category behaviour, highlight, promotional label, see [SponsorBlock](watching.md#sponsorblock) | Everyone, if enabled on the instance |
| Blocked channels | Unblock | Signed in |
| Appearance | Theme (System, Light, Dark), language (English, Deutsch) | Everyone |
| Account | Account type and role, [feed reader link](subscriptions.md#feed-reader-link), password change (local accounts), delete account | Signed in |

Changing the password signs out your other devices.

## Delete your account

Settings > Account > "Delete account". Type your username to confirm, plus your password if the account has one.

!!! danger "This cannot be undone"

    Deleting the account removes your subscriptions, playlists, history, settings and sessions.

- The last admin of an instance cannot delete their account while other users exist.
- If you signed in with SSO, signing in again creates a new, empty account.
